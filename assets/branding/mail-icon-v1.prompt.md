Use case: logo-brand

A rich blue softly rounded square tile occupies about 88% of the canvas width; transparent area outside its rounded outline. Style: Refined minimal vector-like geometry, immaculate edges, mostly flat with only restrained tonal depth at the paper fold. Softly rounded corners. Understated premium desktop productivity identity.
Color: A restrained blue tile with very subtle tonal variation and a clean ivory-white mark.
Constraints: Deliver just the standalone icon, not a presentation sheet or device mockup. Genuine transparent pixels outside the tile. No typography, letters, numbers, watermark, badges, mailbox post, extra objects, borders, glass, metallic finish, heavy shadow, dramatic 3D, tiny lines or decorative sparkles. Do not copy an existing mail application's icon.
