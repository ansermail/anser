# 邮件应用图标 v2

状态：第二版视觉方案，待用户选择。第一版蓝底白信封因接近 Apple Mail 被否定。

方向：暖白底与墨绿折叠纸张，表达邮件归类与收纳。使用内置 image_gen 工具生成，保留透明背景；尚未制作 macOS 发布图标包。

## 最终生成提示词

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: distinctive macOS application icon for an open-source multi-account email app with automatic organization and permanent local mail storage.
Primary request: Create one exceptionally simple, beautiful, original app icon. Previous blue background + white envelope direction was rejected for looking like Apple Mail. This redesign must have a completely different silhouette and color identity.
Subject: One bold abstract folded-paper monogram-like symbol made of two interlocking broad folded ribbons, resembling two neatly nested paper tabs or a compact organized stack of correspondence. A clever single negative-space fold separates the two forms. Give the silhouette a subtle forward movement and memorable asymmetry. It must read as one coherent graphic, not as a collection of objects. It may gently suggest a lowercase m through geometry but must not contain literal lettering. No literal envelope shape.
Style: Sophisticated flat vector-like logo design, bold geometry, carefully judged rounded ends and crisp folds, optical balance, clean edges, restrained and calm. Only two solid symbol colors: deep forest/ink green and a slightly lighter muted jade green fold. No glossy 3D or texture.
Composition: Exactly one standalone icon, straight-on orthographic view. Warm ivory rounded square tile, with generous rounding, centered on a square canvas. Tile occupies 86% of canvas width. Symbol occupies approximately 58% of tile width, with generous balanced whitespace. Transparent pixels outside the tile. High contrast and legible at small Dock sizes.
Constraints: Original identity. No blue background, no white envelope, no postage stamp, no paper airplane, no bird, no circular arrow, no checkmark, no lock, no inbox tray pictogram, no badges, no text, no watermark, no mockup, no multiple options, no presentation board, no tiny details, no decorative lines, no gradients, no shadows. Deliver a polished actual app icon, not a conceptual scene.
```
